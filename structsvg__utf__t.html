Detailed Description

A user transform= as a full 2x3 affine matrix.

A point (x,y) maps to (a*x+c*y+e, b*x+d*y+f). translate, scale, rotate, skewX/Y, and matrix are all representable and closed under composition (priv_utf_compose), so a transform list and nested <g> groups collapse to one of these. The axis-aligned subset (b==0 && c==0) reproduces the legacy scale+translate render byte-for-byte.
