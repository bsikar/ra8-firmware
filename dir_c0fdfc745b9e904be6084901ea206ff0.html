Detailed Description

Two wear-levelling layers over erase-before-write flash, split by which medium and by who wrote them.

ra8_ftl is first-party and serves the on-chip extra MRAM: it wraps an erase-before-write ra8_io_blockdev_t and presents a free-overwrite one upward, so ra8_fs can rewrite a FAT entry in place. LevelX is vendored SOUP and serves the external Octo-SPI NOR (lx_nor_flash_open()), reached through the ra8_fs block-device backend lx_fs_backend_bind() in port/levelx/ (or driven raw, as libs/ra8_cache_store/ does).

Needs ThreadX no yes, unless built via RA8_USE_LEVELX_STANDALONE

Which do I use? It follows from the medium, not from preference: on-chip MRAM -> ra8_ftl; the Octo-SPI part -> LevelX. Neither stacks on the other.
