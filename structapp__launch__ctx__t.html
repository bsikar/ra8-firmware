Detailed Description

Per-app stub state: a display name + lifecycle-call counters.

Each registered app points its ctx at one of these. The vtable callbacks bump the matching counter and log the event, so the self-check can assert the exact focus lifecycle (how many enters/leaves/renders fired) without any display.

Invariant
enters >= leaves while the app is foreground.
