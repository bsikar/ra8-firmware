Detailed Description

Reusable decode buffers so inspection allocates once, not per tile.

ra8_jof_read_tile needs a pixel destination and (for the deflate codec) a stored-stream staging buffer. Both are sized from the atlas geometry and shared across every tile.

Invariant
cell_cap >= tile_w * tile_h * bpp for the atlas being read.
