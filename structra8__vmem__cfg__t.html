Detailed Description

Caller-supplied storage + loader for ra8_vmem_init.

All arrays are caller-owned and must out-live the cache. Carve them once from a tier arena at bring-up (frame storage from SDRAM, the metadata + keys + buckets from DTCM for the hot path).
