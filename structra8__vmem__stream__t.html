Detailed Description

Binds one page-cached object to the byte-stream read adapter.

Caller-owned; populate with ra8_vmem_stream_init and pass its address as the ctx of ra8_vmem_stream_read. Treat the fields as private.

Invariant
frame_bytes == vm->cfg.frame_bytes and frame_bytes > 0.
