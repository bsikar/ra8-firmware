Detailed Description

The two rasters a verification compares, plus their geometries.

ref comes from a single whole-image tile (no tiling exercised); got from the banded encode under test. Equal content is the pass condition.

Invariant
ref != nullptr implies refn > 0; likewise for got/gotn.
