Detailed Description

Binds up to k_ra8_epub_tile_max_sources images to one tile cache.

Owns a ::ra8_tile_cache whose decode-on-miss reads + decodes one JOF tile through ra8_jof_read_tile(). Caller-owned; zero-initialise before ra8_epub_tile_binder_init().

Invariant
Every valid sources[i] has a distinct image_id.
