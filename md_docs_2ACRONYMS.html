RA8D2 Acronym Glossary

Alphabetical glossary of every chip-, board-, and Cortex-M85-specific acronym used in this codebase. Each entry gives a one-line expansion and the HAL driver under libs/ra8_hal/src/ that implements the peripheral (when applicable). Entries grouped by category for browsing, then a flat alphabetical index at the bottom.

Acronym scope: only acronyms that actually appear in libs/ra8_hal/inc/, libs/ra8_hal/src/, or examples/ source. Where an acronym means different things in different vendors' docs, the expansion below is the one Renesas uses in HUM R01UH1065EJ.
