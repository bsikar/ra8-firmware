Chapter Eleven
The Keeper’s Hour

The light came on at dusk, as it always had, and Mara climbed the stair to meet it. Ninety-seven steps, worn shallow in their centers by keepers who had left no names she knew. She counted them the way some people counted prayers — not for the number, but for the rhythm, the small proof that the climb would end.

At the top the lamp waited, patient as an animal. She wound the mechanism and watched the great lens begin its slow turn, throwing its long arm of light out over a sea that gave nothing back.
