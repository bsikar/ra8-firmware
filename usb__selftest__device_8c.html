Detailed Description

Device side of the soak self-test: USBX MSC over a FAT16 MRAM volume.

The USBFS device half of the self-loop: a USBX Mass-Storage class exposing the 1 MiB MRAM window at 0x02000000 as a read-only synthesized FAT16 volume (MRAM.BIN). Owns the USB framework descriptors, the FAT16 sector synthesis, the MSC media callbacks, the USBX device-stack bring-up, and the device worker thread entry (selftest_device_worker, spawned by tx_application_define in main.c). Split out of main.c (see usb_selftest_common.h).
