Detailed Description

Baked parity .epub + golden blob for the M33 compile (#149).

@generated by scripts/gen/rabook_parity_gen.py (make rabook-golden-update); do not hand-edit. The compile_on_m33 CPU1 image compiles s_m33_parity_epub on the Cortex-M33 and the M85 compares the emitted blob to s_m33_parity_golden (byte-identity on the secondary core). Same fixture + golden as the M85 parity gate.
