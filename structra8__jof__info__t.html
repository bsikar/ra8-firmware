Detailed Description

Parsed + validated geometry of one JOF atlas.

Filled by ra8_jof_parse(); callers treat it as read-only after that. tile_cols/tile_rows are the ceil-division grid dimensions; index_off/total_size locate the tile index.

Invariant
tile_count == (uint32_t)tile_cols * tile_rows and index_off + tile_count * 8 + 16 == total_size once parsed.
See also
ra8_jof_parse()
