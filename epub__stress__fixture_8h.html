Baked synthetic large-structure EPUB3 for the #144 pool-stress gate.

60 chapters + 60 manifest resources + an NCX with 60 navPoints + a cover – ~125 archive entries / a large OPF, reproducing a big real book's miniz+tinyxml2 shared- arena pressure in tens of KB. Pure ASCII.
