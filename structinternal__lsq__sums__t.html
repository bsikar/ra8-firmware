Detailed Description

Accumulated least-squares sums driving the calibration solve.

Holds the 11 running sums needed to assemble the 3x3 normal-equation matrix and both right-hand sides for the weighted-least-squares affine fit (Fang & Chang 2007). Bundling them keeps the ra8_touch_cal_compute body compact while preserving bit-exact math.
