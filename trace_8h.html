Detailed Description

Reader access-trace generation + loading for the #147 cache benchmark.

Produces the workload corpus #147 calls for – sequential page-turn flooding, random TOC/bookmark jumps, back-and-forth re-reading (locality), image-tile scroll, a hot-set-plus-scan scan-resistance case, a GB-class "huge book" whose ~7 GiB footprint dwarfs every swept cache, and a mixed realistic session – and loads real traces captured from the reader (ra8_emulator or the EK-RA8D2 over UART) as <object> <page> lines.
